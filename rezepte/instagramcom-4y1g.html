One-Pan Creamy Pesto Pasta

Zutaten

  • One-pan creamy pesto pasta 🍝🌿
  • What you’ll need:
  • 🍝 Tagliatelle or fettuccine
  • 🍅 Cherry tomatoes
  • 🌱 Spinach & basil
  • 🧄 Garlic
  • 🥛 Oat cream + veggie stock
  • 🥄 Green pesto
  • 🧂 Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, chili flakes (optional)
  • Layer everything in one pan, pour in the liquids, and let it cook until the pasta is tender and coated in a silky, creamy pesto sauce. Simple, quick, and full of flavor 🙌
